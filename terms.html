Intended use

rclone-backup is a personal integration maintained by GitHub user niusen for backing up files to the owner's Google Drive account. It is not offered as a public backup or storage service.

Owner responsibilities

The owner is responsible for having permission to transfer the files, protecting access to their devices and Google account, maintaining adequate Google Drive storage, and complying with applicable institutional requirements and service terms.

Backup verification

Transfers can fail or be interrupted. The owner should inspect transfer results, verify backups and retain suitable additional copies. This integration provides no guarantee of uninterrupted availability or data recovery.

Third-party services

Google Drive and rclone are separate services or software with their own applicable terms and licenses. This personal integration is not endorsed by or affiliated with Google or the rclone project.

Stopping use and privacy

The owner can stop using the integration and revoke its Google account access at any time. Existing backups must be deleted separately if desired. See the Privacy Policy for details about data handling.
